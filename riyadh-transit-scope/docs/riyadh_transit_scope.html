Sources

  • Royal Commission for Riyadh City (RCRC) statements via Saudi Press Agency, as reported by Gulf News (6 Sep 2025) and Gulf Business
  • Wikipedia: Riyadh Metro (per-line length, stations, opening; cites RCRC)

  • OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0 — Riyadh Metro route relations, stations, bus routes/stops, district boundaries

RCRC and Riyadh Metro sites block automated access, and no official GTFS feed is published (none is listed in the Mobility Database). Official figures are taken from RCRC statements as reported by SPA and news outlets.

Data build

Station positions, codes, Arabic names and alignments come from OSM route relations and are cross-checked against separately mapped OSM station features. Station names and order follow the official per-line lists. Broken OSM boundary rings are repaired only across gaps under 500 m.

RCRC publishes operating hours and a network headway range (3–7 min) but no per-line timetable. Per-line headways and running speed are modelling assumptions inside the published range; busiest lines (Blue, then Red and Orange, per RCRC ridership) get the shortest headways.
